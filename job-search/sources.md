# Sources — job boards to check

`/discover-jobs` samples from **at least 3 different sources per run** so the
shortlist stays diverse. Always follow an aggregator listing through to the
company's **canonical** ATS/careers page before trusting it (aggregators keep
dead listings up). Edit this list freely.

## Climate / sustainability boards
- Climatebase — https://climatebase.org/jobs
- ClimateTechList — https://climatetechlist.com/jobs
- Climate People — https://www.climatepeople.com/
- Terra.do jobs — https://workfor.climate/ (and Terra.do community board)
- Work in Green — https://www.workingreen.io/

## Mission / nonprofit / policy
- Idealist — https://www.idealist.org/en/jobs
- Environmental Career Center — https://environmentalcareer.com/
- Conservation Job Board — https://www.conservationjobboard.com/
- Tom Calma / policy & gov-adjacent boards as relevant

## General boards (filter hard for fit)
- LinkedIn Jobs — https://www.linkedin.com/jobs
- Built In (climate/impact filter) — https://builtin.com/jobs
- We Work Remotely — https://weworkremotely.com/
- The Muse — https://www.themuse.com/search/

## Company ATS to verify against (canonical)
Greenhouse (`boards.greenhouse.io`), Lever (`jobs.lever.co`), Workday,
Ashby (`jobs.ashbyhq.com`), the company's own `/careers` page.

> The `Apply` link saved to each lead MUST be the canonical posting on the
> company's own ATS/careers site — never the aggregator that surfaced it.
